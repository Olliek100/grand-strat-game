## Dependencies worth knowing
- Focuses and long Ambitions depend on the council spec (who approves them) and on doc 22's tiers (what they unlock).
- Service debuffs depend on Decisions (built) and make shared infrastructure meaningful.
- Activities with stress depend on richer events (stress is fed by events).
- Tech depends on service debuffs (tiers by need).
- Nothing in the "buildable now" group depends on anything in "parked".
