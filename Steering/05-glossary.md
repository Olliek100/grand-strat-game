# Glossary

**Venture** — The core repeating decision pattern: commit resources/people to an action, resolve a success chance shaped by traits/tech/who's sent, get an outcome that feeds back into the trait engine. Applies to expeditions, raids, trade runs, agitation, and diplomatic gambits.

**Trait engine** — The shared system underlying leader traits, faction traits, and culture-derived traits. Every trait has a benefit, a cost, a diplomatic consequence, and an accumulation threshold. Traits can drift or conflict over time.

**Leader trait** — An individual trait belonging to a mortal character; can be inherited or shaped by their history.

**Faction trait** — A trait belonging to a faction as a whole, accumulated from repeated player/AI behaviour (e.g. "Slaver," "Socialist") rather than chosen from a menu.

**Culture stat** — A slow-moving population-level variable (e.g. attention/literacy under a media policy axis) shifted gradually by policy choices; crossing a threshold produces a faction trait. Policy changes are slow and costly to reverse.

**District** — The unit of area on the city map (25–40 per city). Holds owner/influence shares, ruin level, population, grievance, resources, and building slots. Control is a spectrum: unexplored → contested → claimed → secured → developed.

**Regional node** — A settlement, nomad territory, or remnant state on the regional map, represented by a small stat block (strength, wealth, traits, disposition) rather than being internally simulated.

**Agitation** — Funding unrest in a target district to manufacture a revolt, using the same venture pattern. Visibility is opt-in with threshold alerts for non-watchers.

**Grievance** — A per-district and per-internal-faction stat that drives revolt/collapse odds; the target of agitation and the main lever for engineered collapses.

**Collapse cycle** — The probabilistic risk, concentrated around leader death and accumulated grievance, that a mature state fractures into internal factions (Military, Socialists, Conservatives, player's faction, etc.) fighting over district control — the endgame pressure valve, not a scripted rebellion.

**Weighted scoring (AI)** — The rule-based AI approach: each faction scores its available actions using base values plus trait/situation/relationship modifiers and randomness, then takes the highest-scoring action. Same system for player-visible and AI factions.

**Crisis director** — The small set of authored off-map crisis templates (nomad influx, tribute demand, plague, foreign warlord, etc.), triggered by state conditions and telegraphed in advance.

**Playbox** — The first project gate: a greybox prototype of the core loop, built to test whether it's actually fun (not just functional) before committing to full definition/design.

**Vertical slice** — A small section of the game built to final quality across art, audio, UI and systems, used to validate look/feel/pipeline and to extrapolate full production cost. Typically the point where the regional map and crisis director are added.
